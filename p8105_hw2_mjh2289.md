The dataset derives from an observational study investigating the
trajectory of biomarkers associated with Alzheimer’s disease. Importing
the CSV file at baseline required me skipping over the first row in the
file, as that row contained information to describe the data, not data
itself. I then recoded the sex and APOE4 carrier status binary variables
from a numeric 0/1 format to a more descriptive character format
(“Female” and “Male” and “Non-carrier” and “Carrier,” respectively).
Finally, I identified and removed patients with the outcome, Mild
Cognitive Impairment (MCI), at baseline of the study because they were
never at risk of developing MCI in follow-up. Specifically, for patients
who were ever diagnosed with MCI, their age at onset was included, and I
compared their age at onset to their age at baseline. If age at onset
was less than or equal to age at baseline, then the participant was
removed (4 participants).

The clean dataset is tidy and includes 479 participants, 93 of whom
developed MCI in follow-up. Baseline demographic and clinical covariates
include age, sex, years of education, and APOE4 carrier status. The
average age at baseline was 65.0 years, and 210 participants were
female. Of the female participants, 30.0% were APOE4 carriers.
